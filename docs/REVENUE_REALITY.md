| Pre-monetization | Month 0–4 | $0 | Building library; no YPP |

## Path to improve revenue (ordered by ROI)

1. **QA + Security gates** — reduces demonetization and refund churn (highest ROI).
2. **One niche dominance** — 1 channel + 1 SaaS in same niche (SEO + content flywheel).
3. **Kill 90% of ventures** — Revenue Engine defaults are correct; reinvest tokens into SCALE signals only.
4. **Human-likeness audio** — ElevenLabs professional voice clone or licensed voice; beats generic TTS for watch time.
5. **Original IP** — series formats, unique data visualizations, licensed music library.
6. **Regional localization** — Global Agent for DE/BR/IN after English proof.
7. **Paid distribution** — only after LTV > CAC on one product (Month 9+).

## Billion-dollar framing (honest)

A billion-dollar software organization is built on:
- Net revenue retention > 100% on core SKU
- Legal compliance and platform trust
- Sales/support for non-self-serve buyers

AI Squadron can be the **factory** that produces and kills ventures, but the billion-dollar outcome requires **one or few retained winners**, not 450 tabs open. Use the 450-slot grid as a **funnel**, not a target inventory count.
